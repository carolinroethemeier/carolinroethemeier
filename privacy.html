Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit it, GitHub’s servers automatically process technical information that your browser sends, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the visit, the page requested, and your browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the website securely and reliably (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data may be processed in the USA. GitHub has certified to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on which the transfer is based (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub keeps this data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. I do not have access to these logs.

Contact form

If you send me a message through the contact form, I process your name, email address, subject and message in order to reply to you (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR). The form is delivered to my inbox by Formspree, Inc. (USA), which acts as my processor. Formspree stores submissions on servers in the USA; the transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). I delete your message once your request has been dealt with, unless I am legally required to keep it.

No cookies or tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not show advertising.

The fonts on this website are stored on the website’s own server. No connection is made to Google or any other font provider when you visit.

Links to LinkedIn and GitHub

This website links to my profiles on LinkedIn and GitHub but does not embed any of their content. Data is only transferred to these services if you click a link and visit their websites, where their own privacy policies apply.
