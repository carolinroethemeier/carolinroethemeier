Agentic AI is designed for one kind of user - The world is not.

I'm Carolin Roethemeier. I study how cultural background shapes the way employees interact, collaborate, and communicate to work with Agentic AI (multi-modal embedded systems) and how those systems can be designed and governed to work fairly across every location of a multinational company.
